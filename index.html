Final Message to the World

On October 24, 2020, following his victory over Justin Gaethje, Khabib laid his gloves in the center of the Octagon. He promised his mother he would not fight without his father, Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov, by his side. He left as the #1 Pound-for-Pound fighter in the world.
